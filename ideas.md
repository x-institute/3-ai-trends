# AI Signals Atlas — Design Directions

## Approach 1

**Theme Name:** Signal Room

**Very Brief Intro:** A newsroom-meets-research-lab interface built around careful evidence, printed annotations, and visible measurement caveats. It should feel like an annotated analyst’s working brief rather than a generic dashboard.

**Probability:** 0.07

## Approach 2

**Theme Name:** Observatory Console

**Very Brief Intro:** A dark, instrument-like data observatory with luminous traces and precise controls. It emphasizes the feeling of monitoring a complex system in motion.

**Probability:** 0.03

## Approach 3

**Theme Name:** Field Notes Atlas

**Very Brief Intro:** A warm, editorial information atlas that treats each data source as a different terrain—attention, founder intent, and code. Paper textures and margin notes invite careful interpretation rather than instant conclusions.

**Probability:** 0.09

# Chosen Direction: Signal Room

## Design Movement

**Contemporary editorial data journalism** with influences from the Financial Times Visual & Data Journalism desk, scientific field notebooks, and modern research interfaces. It is precise and cerebral without being clinical.

## Core Principles

1. **Evidence is the hero.** Primary metrics, source labels, sample definitions, and caveats are first-class design objects.
2. **Comparison over decoration.** The interface emphasizes contrast between Google attention, YC founder supply, and GitHub building activity rather than decorative AI imagery.
3. **Asymmetric reading flow.** A slim data rail, oversized editorial claims, and staggered card blocks guide the eye through the argument.
4. **Visible uncertainty.** Every metric uses provenance labels and partial-data notes; the site treats limitations as a sign of rigor, not a footnote to hide.

## Color Philosophy

The foundation is **ink-on-newsprint**: charcoal type on a warm bone background supports long-form reading and keeps the data grounded. The three evidence streams use restrained, ownable signal colors—cobalt for public attention, persimmon for startup formation, and verdigris for developer activity. These colors never become gradients; they function as analytic notation.

## Layout Paradigm

The site is organized like a research desk rather than a conventional dashboard. A vertical **Evidence Rail** stays visible at large widths and anchors the three sources. The main canvas shifts between wide editorial statements, compact metric strips, and uneven chart panels. Section dividers resemble tabbed index cards rather than boxes in a centered grid.

## Signature Elements

1. A three-strand **Signal Thread** motif, used as a visual key for Google, YC, and GitHub.
2. **Margin provenance chips** that state source, period, and whether a measurement is partial or retrospective.
3. Fine **field-note rules** and tiny numbered markers that connect conclusions to methods.

## Interaction Philosophy

Controls behave like a research workspace: toggles update the narrative framing and charts together, while source cards reveal methodology rather than simply opening decorative modals. Hover states expose exact values and definitions. The experience rewards inspection.

## Animation

The Signal Thread draws in on initial load with a restrained 650ms stroke-like reveal; section content enters in 40ms staggered steps. Charts should animate only their opacity and vertical transform on first exposure, never continuously pulse. Hovering a stream key subtly emphasizes the corresponding figures throughout the page. Respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Typography System

**DM Serif Display** handles thesis statements, chart labels, and section numerals with confident editorial contrast. **IBM Plex Sans** handles UI, tables, labels, and dense methodological copy. Headlines use tight tracking with oversized, mixed-weight serif forms; operational data uses tabular numeral settings and generous leading.

## Brand Essence

**AI Signals Atlas turns three noisy ecosystems into an inspectable map of what people search, founders sell, and developers build.**

**Personality:** rigorous, observant, humane.

## Brand Voice

Headlines are interpretive but disciplined; CTAs invite inspection rather than conversion; microcopy names uncertainty plainly.

Example lines: “Attention is not adoption. Follow the handoff.”  
“The trend is real; the measurement is qualified.”

## Wordmark & Logo

The wordmark uses a stacked editorial lockup: **AI / Signals / Atlas** with a small three-strand topographic knot at left. The knot is an abstract, text-free emblem that interlaces a cobalt, persimmon, and verdigris line into one bounded mark.

## Signature Brand Color

**Atlas Cobalt — #2859C5.** A deep, disciplined blue used for Google-attention evidence and the core identity mark.
